Accuracy, and what this kit will not do

On bare, hard ground, repeat surveys of the same site agree to within 0.24 m for 90% of the surface and 0.31 m for 95%.

Measured on a validation site on 4 August 2026 by flying it twice and comparing. Bare and hard ground only — vegetation and tree canopy are several times worse. Percentiles, not RMS: the error distribution has heavy tails. Change is quoted relative to the baseline survey, never as absolute levels or heights AOD.

Standard visits fly a DJI Mini 3 Pro. Georeferencing is GNSS-only — no RTK, no PPK, no ground control. These outputs are not survey-grade, must not be used for setting out or boundary determination, and I will not put a number on a stockpile from them. Where a job needs that, I hire an RTK aircraft, set ground control, and the written accuracy statement comes with the file.

How the work runs

  1. Brief. A short call: site, access, induction requirements, what the record is for (progress meetings, programme evidence, claims support, inspection).
  2. Quote. Fixed price in writing, per site, within one working day.
  3. Plan. RAMS supplied, airspace and permissions checked, flight window agreed with the site manager.
  4. Fly. A saved mission — identical waypoints, heights and camera angles every visit, so month three compares to month one.
  5. Process. In-house: orthomosaic, labelled stills, comparison against the previous visit.
  6. Deliver. PDF pack within 48 hours of a successful flight.
